# Case study rules (Divy's portfolio, divy.work)

Rules Divy has set for her case studies. Apply them to every case study, not just this one.

## Structure
- **Visual first.** Open with the hero video, full-bleed, before any title or text. The explanation follows.
- Make the page dynamic: motion that helps people understand (animated diagrams, videos that play in view, scroll-linked effects). It should feel polished, not busy.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: every animation needs a still fallback.

## Visual details
- **No dots, anywhere.** No bullet dots, no orange dots before captions, no dot markers in diagrams or animations. Use lines, numbers or nothing instead.
- Captions under visuals may be orange, but must be **short and precise**: one line saying what the visual shows. Never over-explain.

## Language
- Headings are descriptive, not "catchy". Say plainly what the project is (e.g. "Designing a hand-gesture-based photo gallery").
- The tone should be technical: name the real mechanisms, numbers and APIs. Every claim must be true to the code or to what Divy said; never invent names, purposes or history.
- Don't name side experiments. A short descriptive phrase is enough.

## Branding
- No Apple logos, the iCloud name, or copied Apple UI, including inside screen recordings (blur them).
