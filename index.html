Control it with your hand ✋

  • ✋Move your open palm to glide the cursor over photos.
  • 👌Make an OK sign over a photo (thumb touches index, other three fingers up) to grab it, then move your hand back, away from the screen to enlarge it. Open your fingers to let go.
  • 👆Point with your index finger, then dip the tip to select a photo.
  • ✊Make a fist and move up/down to scroll the grid.
  • ⌨️No camera? Press Tab or any arrow key to reach the photos, then arrows to move, Space to select, Enter to enlarge, Esc to close. Or click to select, double-click to enlarge.
  • 🗑️With photos selected, tap Remove (or press Delete). Undo brings them back.
