Control it with your hand ✋

  • ✋Move your open palm to glide the cursor over photos.
  • 🤏Pinch thumb + index to grab a photo, then pull your hand back to enlarge it.
  • 👆Point with your index finger, then dip the tip to select a photo.
  • ✊Make a fist and move up/down to scroll the grid.
  • ⌨️No camera? Arrow keys to move, Space to select, Enter to enlarge, Esc to close. Or click to select, double-click to enlarge.
